Base64 ⇄ QmFzZTY0 is a tiny, no-nonsense tool for encoding and decoding Base64 right on your phone.

Type into the top field and the Base64 encoding appears instantly below. Type or paste a Base64 string into the bottom field and it is decoded straight back into plain text. Everything happens live as you type — there are no buttons to press and nothing to wait for.

Features:
• Live, two-way conversion — encode and decode as you type
• Completely offline — your text never leaves your device
• No ads, no tracking and no permissions
• Clean, distraction-free interface
• Tiny download

Whether you are a developer decoding a token, a student learning how Base64 works, or you simply need to read an encoded snippet, this app does one job and does it well.
